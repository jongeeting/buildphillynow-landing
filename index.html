Build Philly Now is a Philadelphia economic development think tank dedicated to building more of the things a growing city needs to thrive: housing, transportation, and energy.

Housing, transportation, and energy are the three biggest costs in most families' budgets. Building more of all three creates jobs and opportunity — and brings those costs down. That's our message, and it's why we think pro-growth politics and pro-worker politics belong together.

BPN grew out of Philadelphia 3.0, a nonprofit and PAC formed in 2014 to support independent candidates for Philadelphia City Council. Since 2023, we've been doing something different: spending less time running candidates and more time building coalitions — bringing together the developers, trades, and advocates who want to see Philadelphia grow, and giving them a shared institutional home.

Our board includes leadership from IBEW Local 98 and the Eastern Atlantic States Regional Council of Carpenters. We run the 30,000 Homes housing advocacy table with housing developers, building trades, and mission-driven organizations. We work with the Parker and Shapiro administrations, City Council members, and state legislators who want to make Philadelphia a city that builds. Most pro-housing and pro-growth advocacy talks past organized labor. We don't. That's the point.

One through-line from Philadelphia 3.0 to Build Philly Now is that our success has always depended on building policy networks — connecting people across sectors who share a stake in pro-growth outcomes to each other, and collectively to the decision-makers who can act on them. That convening work is part of what we do: bringing together the strange bedfellows, hosting the conversations that don't happen elsewhere, and building an analytical home that practitioners, advocates, and policymakers across these fields can take seriously. BPN supplies the information and analysis that makes every layer of that network more effective — original reporting for people who need to know what's happening, policy research for people shaping the agenda, and a data dashboard that puts the planning intelligence usually reserved for insiders in the hands of anyone who needs it to do their job.

What we produce

  • Original reporting and analysis on housing, transportation, energy, and economic development — covering City Council, state legislation, city hearings, and the development projects reshaping Philadelphia's neighborhoods.
  • A property data dashboard at map.buildphillynow.org — making the information insiders use to track development accessible to everyone.
  • Policy analysis and advocacy on the issues that determine whether Philadelphia grows or stagnates: zoning reform, office-to-residential conversion, transit investment, energy infrastructure, and the regulatory environment for building.
  • A biweekly policy lunch series connecting policymakers, practitioners, and advocates across our issue areas.
  • A growing coalition of unlikely allies — organized labor, housing advocates, developers, and urbanists — working together on shared policy priorities.
